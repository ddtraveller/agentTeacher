# Candidate schools for a Kru Eng pilot — Thailand

A working list of Thai schools and education institutions that match
the [Kru Eng](../README.md) deployment profile (local-AI English tutor,
PDPA-friendly, runs on the school's own PC). Sorted by fit — the schools
at the top of each category are the realistic first targets; further
down are interesting-but-harder fits.

This is **not** an exhaustive list of Thai schools — there are tens of
thousands. It's a *curated shortlist of named institutions worth a
direct conversation* about hosting Kru Eng or contributing to the
project.

> **Disclaimer:** School fit notes are educated guesses based on
> public information. Hiring practices, age policies, ICT capability,
> and decision-maker openness all change year to year. Verify by
> visiting in person (Chiang Mai) or a 10-minute phone call (elsewhere)
> before relying on this list.

## The tier framework

| Tier | Profile | Why it fits Kru Eng |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | Private language schools, adult/corporate English centers | Owner-led decisions, fast yeses, business-English market already exists, value-add proposition lands cleanly |
| **Tier 2** | Universities (international colleges, TESOL programs, language centers) | Hardware available, research artifact value, but slow procurement and master's-level instructor expectations |
| **Tier 3** | Vocational colleges (อาชีวศึกษา), polytechnics | Underserved by foreign-teacher / foreign-tech offerings; good cultural fit for "practical, hands-on AI" |
| **Tier 4** | Coding bootcamps, digital-nomad-targeted dev schools | High topical fit but most don't sponsor Thai work permits — partnership-only, not employer |
| **Tier 5** | Private K-12 (bilingual / English Program / Cambridge / IB) | Established budgets, real PDPA exposure, but slow procurement and existing tooling partnerships |
| **Tier 6** | Government schools (โรงเรียนรัฐบาล) | Mass impact but require MOE-level paperwork, hardware support, and a head-teacher champion to even start |

### Tier 6 — Thai government schools (โรงเรียนรัฐบาล)

schools become reachable via MOE / OEC partnerships.

## Engagement strategy notes

These aren't all "send an email" targets — different tiers want
different approaches.

| Tier | Best contact method | Best opening |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (private lang schools) | Walk-in. Bring laptop with demo. | "I built this for free, runs on your PC. Can I show you in 10 minutes?" |
| 2 (universities) | Email a specific lecturer in TESOL / Linguistics / CS. Avoid "Dean of..." cold emails. | "Research collaboration on local-AI for language learning. Open-source artifact you can publish on." |
| 3 (vocational) | Call the international affairs office or department head. | "Free practical-AI tooling for your IT/English students." |
| 4 (bootcamps) | DM the founder on Twitter/X or LINE. | "Partnership on AI-deployment curriculum module." |
| 5 (K-12 international) | Email the Head of Secondary School or Head of EAL. | "PDPA-compliant local AI for the English department." Reference the privacy story explicitly. |
| 6 (government K-12) | Skip until you have ≥3 paying / piloting schools as references. | N/A — needs MOE-level intro. |
